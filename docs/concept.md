# Product concept

## Business problem and customer

Korean SME exporters and importers need a transparent view of foreign-currency
position, settlement timing, liquidity gaps, and the assumptions behind hedge
comparisons. Source documents are often inconsistent, while financial and
policy discussions can easily blur simulations with actual product decisions.

KB TradeGuard keeps these concerns separate:

- evidence is extracted into reviewable candidates;
- a human approves every portfolio registration;
- deterministic functions calculate all financial values;
- a read-only advisor explains tool results and retrieves general guidance;
- citations and validation make the basis of each claim inspectable.

## Explainable advisory model

The advisor classifies supported questions, requests missing parameters instead
of guessing, calls controlled tools, and synthesizes a structured answer with
findings, assumptions, considerations, limitations, citations, a follow-up,
and a risk notice. It distinguishes information, simulation, consideration,
and professional decision.

Calculation citations point to immutable structured outputs. Policy citations
point to approved bundled excerpts and preserve issuer, publication date,
retrieval date, source URL, and warnings. Local retrieval does not establish
that a product is currently offered or that a customer is eligible.

The offline provider is the normal no-key path and is never presented as live
AI. A configured provider can add structured intent classification but cannot
replace the deterministic engine or mutate portfolio state.

## Registration and confidence

Every spreadsheet data row becomes its own candidate. Every XLSX sheet is
inspected. The review queue exposes pending, approved, rejected, invalid, and
possible-duplicate states. Fingerprints use multiple normalized business and
source fields, rather than relying only on document reference.

Parsing confidence measures whether the value was technically read. Semantic
mapping confidence measures whether the heading/value maps to the intended
business field. Validation status describes rule conformance. These are review
aids, not calibrated probabilities or proof of document authenticity.

## Decision boundary

The application provides simulations and review considerations. It does not
provide an actual KB quote, current product availability, personalized advice,
eligibility, suitability, a credit rating or limit, loan approval, legal/tax
conclusions, or guaranteed outcomes.
