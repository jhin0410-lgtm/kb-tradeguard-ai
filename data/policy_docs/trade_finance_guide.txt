[section: terminology]
The official Trade Finance Guide describes trade finance as techniques and instruments used to manage payment and delivery risks in international trade. It discusses commercial and country risk, open-account terms, letters of credit, documentary collection, export working capital, export credit insurance, factoring, and related terminology.

[section: working-capital]
The guide explains that exporters offering deferred or open-account terms may need working-capital support across the cash cycle. The appropriate structure depends on transaction documents, payment terms, counterparty and country risk, lender requirements, and current program terms.
